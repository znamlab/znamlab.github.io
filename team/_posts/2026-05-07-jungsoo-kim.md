Jungsoo is a Senior Data Scientist in the Znamenskiy Lab, where he develops scalable pipelines for mapping genes, connections, and functions in neural circuits. He earned a Ph.D. in Neuroscience from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology and a B.S. in Biological Engineering from Cornell University.
